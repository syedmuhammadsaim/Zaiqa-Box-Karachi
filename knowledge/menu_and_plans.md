# ZaiqaBox Karachi - Menu & Subscription Plans

## Overview
ZaiqaBox Karachi is a dedicated corporate meal subscription service delivering fresh, home-cooked, portion-controlled, and nutritionist-reviewed healthy lunch boxes directly to offices and workplaces across Karachi.

## Subscription Plans and Pricing
- **Weekly Trial Plan**:
  - Number of lunches: 5 lunches (Monday to Friday)
  - Price: PKR 3,500
  - Ideal for corporate professionals wanting to experience home-cooked, nutritionist-reviewed lunches for one workweek.

- **Monthly Executive Plan**:
  - Number of lunches: 20 lunches (Monday to Friday across 4 weeks)
  - Price: PKR 12,500
  - Best value subscription for corporate executives looking for a consistent, hassle-free, nutritious office lunch routine.

## Food Quality & Preparation Standards
- All meals are home-cooked using fresh, premium ingredients and hygienic food handling practices.
- Every meal plan is nutritionist-reviewed and carefully portion-controlled to keep corporate professionals energized and productive throughout the workday.
- Prepared with minimal cold-pressed healthy oils, unrefined salt, and balanced macro-nutrients (lean proteins, complex carbohydrates, and fiber).

## Sample Weekly Menu (Monday - Friday)
*Note: This is a sample menu and may change based on fresh seasonal market ingredients. Customization requests are welcome.*
- **Monday**: Tender Chicken Tikka Rice Bowl with fresh kachumber salad and mint yogurt.
- **Tuesday**: Homestyle Daal Palak (lentils & spinach) served with whole-wheat tawa rotis and fresh vegetable salad.
- **Wednesday**: Light Desi Chicken Handi prepared with mild spices, accompanied by whole-wheat chapati and crisp cucumber salad.
- **Thursday**: Spiced Chana Medley with aromatic Matar Pulao and cooling cucumber raita.
- **Friday**: Grilled Fish Fillet or Herb Chicken Cutlets with fragrant steamed basmati rice and roasted vegetables.

## Customization and Dietary Requests
- Customers can request minor customizations such as rotis instead of rice, less spice, or gravy preferences.
- For any medical, diabetic, allergy, or clinical dietary inquiries, our human nutritionist will assist you directly before meal preparation.
