# ZaiqaBox Karachi - Delivery Policy, Zones & Pause Rules

## Delivery Coverage Zones & Delivery Timing
- **Free Delivery Zones**:
  - Defence Housing Authority (DHA) - All phases
  - Clifton - All blocks
  - PECHS (Pakistan Employees Co-operative Housing Society) - All blocks
  - Delivery charge in these zones: PKR 0 (Completely Free)
- **Delivery Time Window**:
  - All lunches are delivered reliably between 12:30 PM and 1:30 PM, right in time for office lunch breaks.

## Deliveries Outside Free Zones
- For corporate offices located outside DHA, Clifton, and PECHS (such as Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Bahadurabad, Tariq Road, Sharah-e-Faisal outside PECHS, Saddar, I.I. Chundrigar Road, Korangi, North Nazimabad, etc.):
  - Delivery charge: PKR 200 per day.
  - Calculated as: Plan Base Price + (PKR 200 × number of delivery days).
  - For Weekly Trial (5 lunches): PKR 3,500 + (PKR 200 × 5 = PKR 1,000) = PKR 4,500 total.
  - For Monthly Executive (20 lunches): PKR 12,500 + (PKR 200 × 20 = PKR 4,000) = PKR 16,500 total.

## Meal Pause and Address Change Policy
- **Pause Deadline**:
  - If you need to pause your lunch or change your office delivery address for the next day, your request must be submitted before 8:00 PM the night before.
- **Why this policy exists**:
  - Fresh market sourcing and morning prep begin before dawn. Strict adherence to the 8:00 PM cutoff ensures zero food wastage and keeps our delivery routes organized.
- **How paused days are handled**:
  - When you pause a lunch before 8:00 PM, that lunch credit is preserved and rolls over forward to extend your subscription period.
