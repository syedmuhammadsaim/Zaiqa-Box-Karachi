# ZaiqaBox Karachi - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## What subscription plans are available and what are the prices?
ZaiqaBox Karachi provides two straightforward corporate plans:
1. Weekly Trial: 5 lunches (Monday to Friday) for PKR 3,500.
2. Monthly Executive: 20 lunches (Monday to Friday for 4 weeks) for PKR 12,500.
All meals are fresh, home-cooked, nutritionist-reviewed, and portion-controlled for working professionals.

## Which areas receive free delivery, and what time will my lunch arrive?
We offer free delivery in DHA, Clifton, and PECHS. All lunches are delivered between 12:30 PM and 1:30 PM, ensuring warm and fresh food for your corporate lunch hour.

## Do you deliver outside DHA, Clifton, and PECHS?
Yes, we deliver across Karachi. For areas outside our free zones (such as Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Saddar, I.I. Chundrigar Road, Bahadurabad, etc.), a nominal delivery fee of PKR 200 per day applies.

## What is your pause or address change policy?
If you have a meeting off-site, travel plans, or need to pause or change your delivery address, you must inform us via WhatsApp before 8:00 PM the night prior. Requests received after 8:00 PM cannot be accommodated for the next day's meal as cooking and routing preparations begin early in the morning. Paused meals are not lost; they roll over to subsequent days.

## Can I customize my meals?
Yes, minor customizations (such as preference for whole-wheat rotis over rice, or milder spice levels) can be accommodated. You can mention your preference when confirming your order on WhatsApp.

## How do I place an order?
You can choose your plan and location on our website, calculate your price, and click "Order on WhatsApp" to send us a pre-filled WhatsApp message. Our customer support team will immediately confirm your subscription and delivery location.

## Is this food suitable for diabetes, weight loss, allergies, or medical conditions?
ZaiqaBox meals are portion-controlled, home-cooked, and nutritionist-reviewed for healthy office professionals. However, we never make or imply any medical, diabetic, or weight-loss claim or guarantee. For any medical or dietary questions, our human nutritionist will assist you. If you have specific dietary restrictions, allergies, or medical concerns, please connect with our human nutritionist directly on WhatsApp.
